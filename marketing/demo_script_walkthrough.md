# SkillCascade — Product Walkthrough Demo
# ~90 seconds, calm product tour, "let me show you around"

---

**[OPEN — 0 to 8 seconds]**
*Screen: Landing page → click "Dashboard" → Home dashboard loads*

"Let me walk you through SkillCascade — a developmental assessment and clinical planning tool designed specifically for BCBAs."

---

**[ASSESSMENT — 8 to 25 seconds]**
*Screen: Click into Assessment view → show a sub-area with skills → rate a couple skills*

"At the core is a two-hundred-sixty skill assessment across nine developmental domains — from regulation and self-awareness, all the way through communication, social understanding, and safety skills. Each skill gets rated on a simple scale — not present, needs work, developing, or solid. You can work through it section by section, or jump to any domain."

---

**[VISUALIZATIONS — 25 to 50 seconds]**
*Screen: Click Sunburst → show it lighting up → click Radar → show domain comparison → click Cascade view → show the cascade analysis*

"Once you start assessing, the data comes alive. The sunburst gives you an instant visual of where your client stands across every domain and sub-area. The radar chart lets you compare domains at a glance — you can immediately see where the gaps are.

But the real insight is in the cascade view. SkillCascade maps developmental dependencies — so you can see exactly which foundation gaps in regulation or executive function are limiting progress in communication or social skills upstream. This is the clinical picture that's hard to see on paper."

---

**[GOAL ENGINE — 50 to 65 seconds]**
*Screen: Click Goals → show prioritized skills → Foundation Gaps, Ready to Target, Blocked sections*

"The goal engine takes your assessment data and automatically prioritizes what to target. Foundation gaps that are blocking multiple domains show up first. Skills that are ready to work on — because their prerequisites are met — come next. You're not guessing what to target. The developmental hierarchy tells you."

---

**[PER-SKILL GOALS — 65 to 80 seconds]**
*Screen: Tap target icon on a skill → instant goal appears → show mastery criteria → tap Enhance with AI → show enhanced result with clinical notes*

"Tap any skill and you get an instant goal template — already formatted with your preferred mastery criteria. That's the quick version. But if you want the full picture, tap 'Enhance with AI' — and it analyzes the entire client profile. Prerequisites, ceiling constraints, the cascade model — and gives you a goal with clinical reasoning for why it fits this specific client."

---

**[AI TOOLS — 80 to 95 seconds]**
*Screen: Open AI Tools panel → show the 8 tools → click Report Writer → show a quick response*

"There's also a full suite of AI clinical tools — a report writer for deficit summaries and observations, a BIP creator, a goal writer, a goal analyzer that checks if your goals are truly behavioral, and an operational definition generator. All of them are aware of your client's assessment data, so everything they produce is specific to the client you're working with."

---

**[CLOSE — 95 to 105 seconds]**
*Screen: Back to home dashboard showing the completion ring, domain cards, sparklines*

"SkillCascade gives you the assessment, the visualization, the clinical prioritization, and the goal writing — all in one place. Try it free for fourteen days at skillcascade.com."
