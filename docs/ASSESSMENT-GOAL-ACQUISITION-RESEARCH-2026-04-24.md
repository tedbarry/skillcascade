# Assessment Goal Acquisition Research

Status: Draft v1
Date: 2026-04-24
Purpose: Determine where recommended goals, intervention guidance, or usable planning outputs actually live for target assessment systems.

## Executive Conclusion

There is a way, but it is not one single way.

The evidence supports four different acquisition patterns:

1. Some systems generate report-level intervention guidance or intervention recommendations.
2. Some systems provide intervention planners or item-level intervention tips inside proprietary software.
3. Some systems embed IEP goals, program worksheets, or curriculum/teaching content in apps, guides, or digital platforms.
4. Some systems only provide scored domains/subscales and require SkillCascade to do the goal translation.

The mistake would be treating every assessment as if it exposes a public downloadable goal bank.

## Official Evidence

### Vineland-3

- Pearson product page: Vineland-3 "provides valuable information for developing educational and treatment plans."
- Pearson sample report: pages 12-27 are labeled `Intervention Guidance` and are omitted from the public sample for copyright protection.

Implication:

- There is real intervention-guidance content in at least some official Vineland-3 report outputs.
- The problem is not that it does not exist.
- The problem is access and format.

### VB-MAPP

- AVB says the VB-MAPP Guide includes suggestions for IEP goals and intervention programs.
- AVB says the VB-MAPP App provides automatic reporting of results with IEP goals.

Implication:

- VB-MAPP is one of the clearest sources for directly assessment-linked goal output.

### ABLLS-R

- The ABLLS-R Guide says it helps users develop IEP goals and objectives.
- WebABLLS says reports correlate directly with learner assessment scores.
- WebABLLS offers Program Worksheet and Progress Report outputs.

Implication:

- ABLLS-R likely exposes usable goal/program content through its guide and reporting workflow, even if not as a simple public goal list.

### AFLS

- WPS says AFLS is an instructional blueprint with task analyses and teaching suggestions.

Implication:

- AFLS is likely best as a structured curriculum and task-analysis source rather than a list of polished payer-ready goals.

### PEAK

- PEAK says each module contains a full 184-item skill assessment and a 184-item curriculum.

Implication:

- PEAK provides rich source material, but canonical goals will likely need to be derived from curriculum items and module structure.

### Essential for Living

- EFL says it contains over 3,100 sequenced life skills.
- EFL also offers an app, learner scoring manuals, and a teaching manual with procedures.

Implication:

- EFL is a rich source for functional-life-skill targets and teaching guidance.
- It is likely not a prewritten insurance-goal bank in the format SkillCascade ultimately needs.

### ABAS-3

- WPS says the ABAS-3 Intervention Planner creates customized therapy plans based on adaptive deficits.
- WPS says the planner includes straightforward intervention activities for each ABAS-3 item.
- WPS OES user guide says ABAS-3 supports Intervention Reports and Interpretive Reports.

Implication:

- ABAS-3 is one of the strongest sources for item-linked intervention guidance and may be one of the best adaptive-behavior pipelines to leverage.

### BASC-3

- Pearson offers a BASC-3 Intervention Guide.
- Pearson sample report shows explicit `Intervention Recommendations` and `Target Behaviors for Intervention`.

Implication:

- BASC-3 has direct recommendation output, but it is targeted more toward behavioral/emotional intervention than broad skill acquisition.

### SRS-2

- WPS says SRS-2 treatment subscales help guide intervention.
- WPS OES user guide lists SRS-2 for Score Reports and Progress Monitoring Reports, but not for Intervention Reports.

Implication:

- SRS-2 provides intervention direction, but not necessarily a recommendation report with ready-made goals.

### DABS

- AAIDD frames DABS as a diagnostic adaptive behavior assessment scored on its platform.

Implication:

- DABS should be treated as a diagnostic/supporting input, not a core goal source.

## Local Report Reality Check

### Vineland corpus

The de-identified local Vineland review suggests:

- the saved reports are readable and clinically useful
- they contain score summaries, subdomains, and parent-report content
- they do not appear to include the explicit `Intervention Guidance` section in the stored local PDFs reviewed so far

Best inference:

- your saved Vineland PDFs are probably not the fullest report flavor that contains the intervention-guidance pages, or those pages were not included in the exported artifacts you saved

### SRS-2 corpus

The de-identified local SRS-2 review suggests:

- the saved PDFs are score reports
- they contain treatment subscales
- they do not appear to include explicit recommended-goal pages

Best inference:

- your stored SRS-2 exports are useful for goal weighting and subdomain prioritization, but not direct goal extraction

## Local Source Inventory Check

A private filename-level inventory across local client folders suggests:

- no local VB-MAPP files found by filename
- no local ABAS-3 files found by filename
- 12 local ABLLS-R spreadsheet files found
- 3 local AFLS spreadsheet files found
- 1 BASC-related text file found, but it does not appear to be an intervention report export

### What that likely means

- the best local sources already in hand are ABLLS-R and AFLS
- the best external or platform-access paths are likely VB-MAPP App, Vineland-3 intervention-guidance outputs, ABAS-3 Intervention Planner outputs, and BASC-3 intervention-recommendation reports

### Additional local observation

The AFLS spreadsheets appear to function more like structured skill inventories and present-level summaries than polished ready-made goal exports.

Implication:

- AFLS is likely highly usable, but through translation from assessed skill sets and present-level structures into canonical SkillCascade goals

## Most Viable Acquisition Paths

### Tier 1: Best direct sources

1. VB-MAPP App report output with IEP goals
2. VB-MAPP Guide placement and IEP goal sections
3. ABAS-3 Intervention Planner / OES Intervention Report
4. BASC-3 reports with intervention recommendations
5. Vineland-3 full report outputs that include Intervention Guidance pages

### Tier 2: Strong structured sources requiring translation

1. ABLLS-R Guide + WebABLLS Program Worksheet outputs
2. AFLS guide/protocol task analyses and teaching suggestions
3. PEAK module assessments and curriculum
4. Essential for Living app/manuals/teaching procedures

### Tier 3: Best as weighting and interpretation inputs

1. SRS-2 treatment subscales
2. DABS diagnostic outputs

## Practical Recommendation

Do not keep researching this as if the answer is "find one big online goal library."

The better move is:

1. pursue the most direct recommendation-output systems first
2. identify what you already have access to through purchased platforms/manuals
3. build import paths for report outputs where available
4. build crosswalk translators for curriculum-style systems where goals are implied rather than explicitly packaged

## Best Next Targets

3. ABAS-3 Intervention Planner outputs
4. ABLLS-R / WebABLLS Program Worksheet outputs
