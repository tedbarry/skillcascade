[2026-05-20] **PHI-safe productization now has a written runbook and secret-handling baseline** - The productization track is explicitly AWS-first for PHI/ePHI under an active BAA, with Cloudflare/public/admin/desktop surfaces treated as non-PHI unless separately covered and constrained. The runbook requires review-before-final clinical outputs, approval gates for report/tree/external mutations, Bedrock-only PHI-capable AI, and no raw Codex-skill packaging as the sellable product.
